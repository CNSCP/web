Privacy notice

Privacy Notice for the CP Registry

What the Connection Profile Registry at cp.cnscp.io collects from the people who use it, what it makes public, and what it never holds.

What the Registry is

The Connection Profile Registry at cp.cnscp.io is the public record of CNS/CP Connection Profiles: which organization holds each Prefix, which Profiles have been published beneath it, and every change to either, in order. It is operated by Padi, Inc. as the CNS/CP operator. This notice describes what the Registry collects from the people who use it, what it makes public, and what it never holds. It applies to cp.cnscp.io; the pages at cnscp.io collect nothing beyond ordinary web server logs.

Reading the Registry

Resolving a Profile, browsing the catalog, or following the public feed needs no account and sets no cookie. Like any web server, the Registry logs the address, browser and page of each request to operate and protect the service; those logs are kept briefly and are not used for anything else.

Signing in

To register or publish you need an account, and an account is created by signing in with Google or GitHub. The Registry does not ask for, receive, or store a password. From the provider it receives the provider’s identifier for your account, your email address and whether the provider has verified it, and your display name. It asks for nothing else, and it keeps no token that would let it act on your Google or GitHub account afterwards. Your relationship with the provider is governed by that provider’s own privacy policy.

Signing in sets one cookie so the Registry knows it is you on its account pages. That cookie is used for nothing else, is not shared, and expires by itself.

What is stored about an account

Your email address and display name; the provider identities you have signed in with, and when; the organizations you belong to and your role in each; the access tokens you have minted — stored only as one-way hashes, with the label you gave each, what it may do, and when it was created, last used and revoked; and the browser sessions you have opened, likewise hashed. A token is shown to you once, when you mint it, and cannot be recovered from the Registry afterwards.

What is public

The Registry is a public record, and publishing to it is a public act. Everything published is public and permanent: Profiles and their versions; the organization holding each Prefix and any change of holder; registrations, publications, deprecations and changes of stewardship. Each such act is recorded with the email address of the person responsible for it, and that record is part of the public feed that anyone may read, copy and verify. If you publish, your email address becomes part of the permanent public record of that publication.

Accounts, memberships and tokens are not public. They appear in the public feed only as cryptographic hashes, so that the record can be verified as complete without its contents being readable.

Retention and removal

Published content and the record of public acts are permanent by design: a published Profile is never altered or deleted, only deprecated, and the audit record is append-only. You may close your account at any time by writing to info@cnscp.io; that removes your sign-in, sessions and tokens, and ends your memberships, but does not remove the public record of acts performed under your name, because others may have relied on them.

Sharing

The Registry does not sell, rent or trade information about its users, and shows no advertising. Information is shared only with the sign-in providers you choose to use, with the hosting provider on whose infrastructure the Registry runs (Google Cloud), and where the law requires it.

Changes and contact

This notice may change as the Registry grows; the current version is always at this address, dated. Questions about it go to info@cnscp.io.

Effective 13 September 2026.
