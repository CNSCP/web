CNS/CP Licensing Information

Openly licensed to promote widespread, royalty-free use — with one namespace, so every implementation stays interoperable.

The Specification

The CNS/CP specification was developed by and is the intellectual property of Padi, Inc. It is Padi’s intention to promote and encourage the widespread and royalty-free use of CNS/CP by the public. To that end, Padi is committed to developing a framework to license the specification on a perpetual, royalty-free, and non-revocable basis.

Software License

All open-source software provided by Padi, Inc. related to CNS/CP is licensed under a license based on Apache 2.0.

The purpose of this license is to ensure that all uses of CNS/CP remain interoperable with each other by mandating a single namespace for the names of Connection Profiles.
