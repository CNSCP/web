Terms of Use for the CP Registry

The terms on which the Connection Profile Registry at cp.cnscp.io is offered and used.

The service

The Connection Profile Registry at cp.cnscp.io is the canonical record of CNS/CP Connection Profiles, operated by Padi, Inc. as the CNS/CP operator. By using it you accept these terms. Reading the Registry is open to all and needs no account; registering names and publishing Profiles needs an account and a membership of the organization that holds the relevant Prefix.

Names and Prefixes

Top Level Prefixes are allocated under the CNS/CP specification and the Registry’s published policy. Each Prefix has one holder, an organization, and the holder’s members may register names and publish Profiles beneath it. The operator may withhold Prefixes for infrastructure, documentary or policy reasons, may release a Prefix to its evident owner, and may transfer or suspend a Prefix under the published policy; every such act is recorded with its reasons in the public audit record. Holding a Prefix does not confer a trademark or any right beyond the ability to publish beneath it in this Registry.

Publication is permanent

A published Profile version is immutable. It cannot be altered or deleted by you, by the operator, or by anyone; a version that turns out to be wrong can only be deprecated, and a new version or a new name published in its place. You should publish only what you are entitled to publish and only what you are content to have public, permanent, and relied upon by others. The Registry offers a rehearsal of every publication that changes nothing; use it.

What you grant by publishing

By publishing a Profile you confirm that you have the right to do so, and you license it to everyone under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0). The operator, every Registry instance that follows the public feed, every reader and every implementation may store, serve, copy, distribute, verify and build on it, for any purpose, with attribution to its publisher. The licence is irrevocable, which matches the record: a published version cannot be withdrawn. What the licence does not carry is the name — an adaptation of a Profile is a new Profile, and a name is registered here under its Prefix holder, not by copying. A Profile is a contract meant to be read by anyone who may connect to it; nothing published can be confidential.

Accounts and tokens

Your account is yours alone. Tokens you mint act as you, and acts done with them are recorded under your name; keep them secret, and revoke any token that may have been exposed — revocation is immediate. You are responsible for what your tokens do, including tokens you mint for an assistant or an automated process, which act for you and are recorded as doing so.

Conduct

Do not misrepresent who you are or which organization you act for; do not register names or seek Prefixes you have no claim to; do not publish content that is unlawful, that infringes another’s rights, or that is designed to mislead; and do not attempt to disrupt the service or to alter the record by any means other than the acts the Registry provides. The operator may revoke credentials, end memberships or suspend a Prefix for breach of these terms, with the reasons recorded.

The public record

Every act on the Registry is written to an append-only, cryptographically linked audit record. Public acts — registrations, publications, deprecations, stewardship changes, allocations and transfers — are served in full in the public feed, including the email address of the person responsible. Accounts, memberships and credentials appear in the feed only as hashes. See the privacy notice.
